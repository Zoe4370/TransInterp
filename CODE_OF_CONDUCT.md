# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in TransInterp a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, expertise, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate with care, assume good intent while addressing impact, welcome correction, and focus criticism on ideas, evidence, and code. Research discussions should make room for uncertainty and disagreement.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, intimidation, sexualized conduct, and knowingly deceptive presentation of research are not acceptable. Private information must not be shared without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainer **Zoe Faith Gumise** may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. To report a concern, contact Zoe privately at **gumisezoe@gmail.com** or use the security reporting process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
